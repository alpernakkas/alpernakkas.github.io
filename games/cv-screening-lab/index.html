Who makes your shortlist?

Screen eight fictional CVs for a project manager role. Change the criteria, inspect the evidence, and challenge the proposed shortlist.

Rule-based agent simulation. This browser demo follows programmed screening steps. It uses no language model or AI API, sends no CV data to a service, and incurs no AI API charges. All candidates and employers are fictional.
01 / Set the policy

Screening criteria

Role: Project manager for Meridian’s retail expansion. Coordinate delivery schedules, budgets, and stakeholders. A certification is optional unless your team makes it required.

Synonyms use a fixed dictionary, not AI understanding. Neither mode verifies the quality of a claim.

Scoring weights

Weights normalize to 100%. Experience reaches full credit at 6 years. Other criteria get credit when the chosen rule finds evidence. Unknown evidence earns no confirmed points, with possible points shown separately.

Your review rationale

Select candidates after checking the evidence. The proposal is advisory.

02 / Inspect the workflow

What the simulator does

No live AI connection
  1. Read your requirements and scoring weights.
  2. Extract experience and match evidence in each CV.
  3. Check required criteria, calculate scores, and flag unknowns.
  4. Propose a shortlist of candidates meeting the stated requirements.
  5. Show evidence for your review. You retain the selection decision.

A future AI agent could choose tools to perform these steps and explain results in its own words. Here, the workflow and explanations are programmed and repeatable.

Run the workflow to see scores and a proposed shortlist.

03 / Review the proposal

Screening results

Score is a rule-based evidence measure, not a hiring probability. The range shows confirmed score to possible score if unknown criteria receive full credit. Candidates needing clarification stay outside the automatic proposal.

See this run’s tool activity and scoring rules

    Confirmed score = 100 × sum(weight × criterion credit) / total weight. Experience credit = min(years / 6, 1). Each evidenced skill or certification has credit 1. Explicitly absent criteria have credit 0. Unstated criteria remain unknown. Equal scores share a rank. A candidate ID breaks ties only when a fixed shortlist size requires it; review that boundary.

    04 / Check the evidence

    Candidate CVs and explanations

    Open a CV before running, or inspect its evidence after screening. No real applicant data is used. Statements in a CV are claims to verify.

    05 / Challenge the system

    Classroom experiments

    1. Run literal matching with PMP required. Which candidates need clarification?
    2. Remove the PMP requirement and run again. Did a qualification proxy exclude someone with relevant delivery evidence?
    3. Switch to defined synonyms. Compare candidate C02’s evidence and ranking with the first run.
    4. Increase the budget weight. Does your shortlist change? Explain why your weights suit the role.
    5. Review an uncertain candidate manually. Identify the question you would ask before making a decision.

    Discussion

    Who owns the criteria? What can keyword evidence actually establish? How should missing information and ties affect human review?
